# AdventureWorks Business Dashboard

An interactive dashboard I built for the AdventureWorks sample data as my own project in the Information Visualization course at the University of Bayreuth (summer semester 2026). It brings sales, customers and stock together in one place, and everything can be filtered by period, country and product category.

Customers are grouped into value tiers based on how much they spend and how actively they buy. On the stock side, the dashboard compares what is in stock with the demand of the last 180 days and points out products that should be restocked or are clearly overstocked.

**[Open the dashboard](https://burmand.github.io/adventureworks-dashboard/adventureworks_dashboard.html)**

Built with Python, pandas and Plotly.
